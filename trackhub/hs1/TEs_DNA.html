DNA elements

RepeatMasker-annotated DNA elements. Item name is the subfamily, item score is divergence in substitutions per 1000 bp (observed range 0-480). Note that the paper reports enrichment as the ratio of the observed to a 500-permutation random odds ratio, because the raw odds ratio scales with element length.
