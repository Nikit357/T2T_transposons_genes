Interferon-alpha domain

The 220 kb domain on chromosome 9 (chr9:21,150,692-21,370,055) containing IFNA4, IFNA6, IFNA7, IFNA10, IFNA14, IFNA16, IFNA17, IFNA21, IFNA22P, IFNA5, IFNW1 and KLHL9. It holds 175 TEs, of which 77 are L1 copies from 36 subfamilies at a mean divergence of 135.7 against a genome-wide L1 mean of 188.2. The deficit survives null windows matched for L1 count (empirical p = 0.006) and for gene density (p = 0.002).
