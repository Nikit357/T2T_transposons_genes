T2T TEs by classAll 3,709,429 RepeatMasker elements, one subtrack per class (LINE, SINE, LTR, DNA, SVA, Helitron). Item name is the subfamily; item score is divergence in substitutions per 1000 bp (observed range 0–480; higher means older).
